About Us

What is Telecall AI?

A complete AI-powered telecalling platform designed to automate customer conversations and help businesses manage large-scale calling operations.

Turning phone calls into intelligent conversations.

Traditional telecalling requires large teams of operators to repeatedly call customers, understand their responses and record the results.

Telecall AI automates this workflow using Python, Django, speech recognition, AI language models and text-to-speech technology.

The system can automatically call customers, listen to the customer voice, understand the conversation and respond using an AI-generated voice.

Technology Flow

How Telecall AI Works

The system connects telephony, speech recognition, AI and voice synthesis into one workflow.

1

Auto Dial

System selects a customer from the call queue and initiates the phone call.

2

Customer Voice

Customer-side call audio is captured for speech processing.

3

Whisper

Speech recognition converts the customer's voice into text.

4

AI Brain

The AI processes the customer message and generates an appropriate response.

5

AI Voice

The generated response is converted into speech and returned to the call.
